## 双语文章 · A Small Town Learns to Work Together / 一个小镇学会携手合作

> 读法: 先读英文段落,遇到**加粗**的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

In a small town in the north of the **state**, people once believed that nothing would ever **happen** there. The **local** **economic** **situation** was bad: the old **oil** **industry** had closed, many families had to **lose** their jobs, and young people left for the city. **However**, things began to change when a new **leader** arrived.

在这个**州**北部的一个小镇上,人们曾以为这里永远不会**发生**什么事。**当地的****经济****状况**很糟:老旧的**石油****产业**已经关闭,许多家庭不得不**失去**工作,年轻人都去了城里。**然而**,一位新**领导者**到来后,情况开始改变。

Her name was Ms. Lee, and she had been a **former** **director** of an **education** **program** in the **federal** **government**. She **actually** knew very little about the town's **history**, but she had a strong **sense** of what a **community** needs. "We **might** not have much money," she said in her first **public** speech, "but every person here can **offer** something. We need to **consider** what we can **create** together, not just what we have lost."

她叫李女士,**曾经**在**联邦****政府**的一个**教育****项目**中担任**主任**。她其实(**actually**)对小镇的**历史**了解很少,但她很清楚一个**社区**需要什么。"我们**也许**没有多少钱,"她在第一次**公开**演讲中说,"但这里的每个人都能**提供**些什么。我们需要**考虑**能一起**创造**什么,而不只是我们失去了什么。"

Her first step was **research**. She asked a **team** of volunteers to collect **data** about every **area** of town life: **business**, health, sports and **art**. The **result** was surprising. The **rate** of young people without work was high, but so was their **interest** in music, games and design. One **kid**, a fourteen-year-old **player** on the school basketball **team**, told her, "There is no **space** for us. There is nowhere to go after school."

她的第一步是**调研**。她请一支志愿者**团队**收集小镇生活各个**领域**的**数据**:**商业**、健康、体育和**艺术**。**结果**令人惊讶。没有工作的年轻人**比例**很高,但他们对音乐、游戏和设计的**兴趣**也很高。一个十四岁的**孩子**,学校篮球队的**队员**,对她说:"我们没有**空间**,放学后无处可去。"

This became the **point** of her **project**. She decided to turn an old **court** building near the town **base** into a youth center. The **process** was not easy. Some **political** groups were **against** the idea; they said it was a waste of **tax** money. A few **police** officers worried that the center would attract **drug** dealers. Ms. Lee did not **explain** her plan only once; she **continued** to meet people until they understood her **view**.

这成了她这个**项目**的**重点**。她**决定**把镇中心(**base** 基地)附近的一座旧**法院**大楼改造成青年中心。这个**过程**并不容易。一些**政治**团体**反对**这个想法,认为这是浪费**税**款。几位**警察**担心中心会吸引**毒品**贩子。李女士并没有只**解释**一次就算了,她**持续**与人们见面,直到他们理解她的**观点**。

"The **police** have a **role** here too," she said. "This is not about **power** or **control**. It is about **support**. Every **human** being needs a place to belong." **Finally**, the town council held a vote. The **decision** was close, but the center **won**. **Within** six months, the building was open.

"**警察**在这里也有自己的**角色**,"她说。"这不是关于**权力**或**控制**,而是关于**支持**。每个**人**都需要一个归属的地方。"**最后**,镇议会进行了投票。**决定**的票数很接近,但中心胜出(**win**)了。不到(**within**)六个月,大楼就开放了。

The center **included** a music room, a small **movie** theater and an **art** studio. Every month there was a special **event**: a concert, a painting show, or a basketball game between kids and **police** officers. One **couple** who ran a local café **added** free food on Fridays. An **international** student from Korea taught a **course** in digital design. The **official** **site** of the center showed an **image** of a mural the kids had painted, and soon people from other towns came to see it.

中心**包括**一间音乐室、一个小**电影**院和一间**艺术**工作室。每个月都有一次特别**活动**:音乐会、画展,或者孩子们和**警察**之间的篮球赛。一对经营当地咖啡馆的**夫妇****增加**了每周五的免费餐。一位来自韩国的**国际**学生开了一门数字设计**课程**。中心的**官方****网站**上展示了一幅孩子们画的壁画**图片**,很快其他城镇的人也来参观。

Not everything was perfect. A **major** storm **hit** the town in the second winter, and the roof was damaged. There was a fire **test** that the building failed, and one **patient** in the nearby hospital complained about the noise. These problems **required** extra **effort**, but the **whole** town came to help. Even the **president** of the **national** youth **society** sent a letter of **support**.

并非一切都很完美。第二个冬天,一场**大**风暴**袭击**了小镇,屋顶受损。大楼没通过一次消防**检查**,附近医院的一位**病人**也抱怨噪音。这些问题**需要**额外的**努力**,但**整个**小镇都来帮忙。就连**全国**青年**协会**的**主席**也寄来了一封**支持**信。

What **effect** did the center have? The **record** is clear. The number of young people involved in crime dropped, and some even started small **businesses**. One former **drug** user became a **model** for other kids, giving talks about his **experience**. **Especially** important was the new **relationship** between the youth and the **police**. Officers who once saw kids as a problem now **describe** them as partners. Some kids even said they wanted to join the **military** or the **police** **force** one day.

中心带来了什么**影响**?**记录**很清楚。参与犯罪的年轻人数量下降了,有些人甚至开始做小**生意**。一位曾经吸**毒**的年轻人成了其他孩子的**榜样**,讲述自己的**经历**。**尤其**重要的是年轻人和**警察**之间的新**关系**。曾经把孩子们看作问题的警官,现在把他们**描述**为伙伴。有些孩子甚至说将来想参加**军队**或**警察**队伍(**force**)。

The town's story became a **recent** **issue** in the **national** news. Reporters asked Ms. Lee what made the **difference**. She said, "Many people think change needs a **war** against problems. I disagree. Change needs people to **follow** a shared goal. The **fact** is, our town had all the **value** it needed; we just had to **reach** it."

小镇的故事成了**全国**新闻中一个**最近**的**话题**。记者问李女士是什么带来了**不同**。她说:"很多人以为改变需要对问题发动一场**战争**。我不同意。改变需要人们**追随**一个共同的目标。**事实**是,我们小镇本来就拥有它所需要的全部**价值**,我们只是需要去**触及**它。"

**Perhaps** the center will not **remain** the same forever. **Probably** it will grow and change, like any living **system**. **Although** some problems still exist, there is **less** fear and more hope. A small **line** of words is now painted above the door: "**Allow** everyone to **lead**." It is a **certain** reminder that **social** change does not always start in a big **nation**'s capital. Sometimes it starts in a small town, with one person who is willing to **act**—and with an **action** that **produces** results.

**也许**这个中心不会永远**保持**不变。它**很可能**会像任何有生命的**系统**一样成长和变化。**虽然**一些问题依然存在,但恐惧**少**了,希望多了。现在门上方漆着一**行**小字:"**让**每个人都能**引领**。"这是一个**确定无疑**的提醒:**社会**变革并不总是从一个大**国**的首都开始。有时它从一个小镇开始,从一个愿意行动的人开始,从一次带来成果(**produce**)的**行动**开始。

As for the **death** of the old town? It never came. **Later**, when a reporter returned, she found the **phone** at the youth center ringing all day. Parents wanted to sign up their kids, and businesses wanted to sponsor the next **event**. The town was alive again. It had found its **figure** in the world: not a place where nothing happens, but a place where people make things happen.

至于老镇的**消亡**?它从未到来。**后来**,当一位记者再次来访时,她发现青年中心的**电话**整天响个不停。家长们想给孩子报名,商家们想赞助下一次**活动**。小镇重新焕发了生机。它在世界上找到了自己的**形象**(**figure**):不再是一个什么都不会发生的地方,而是一个人们让事情发生的地方。
